## Free under the AGPL

Everyone may use, study, change and run SutraFlow free of charge, for any purpose, in-house or for customers, as long as the AGPL's conditions are met. The most important:

- **If you change SutraFlow** (or build software that includes or is derived from it) **and people use it over a network**, for example your staff, customers or partners reaching the designer, the Admin Console or services on the gateway, you must offer those users the complete source code of your changed version, under the AGPL.
- **If you distribute SutraFlow** (changed or not), for example in an installer, a container image or an appliance, you must pass on the source code and the AGPL with it.
- Copyright and licence notices must be kept.

Running SutraFlow **unchanged**, as published, needs no commercial licence: there are no changes to share.

## When you need a commercial licence

You need a commercial licence when you want to use SutraFlow **without** meeting the AGPL's conditions, for example:

- **In-house deployments with private changes**: you change SutraFlow (new activities, connectors, fixes, branding, integration into your platform) and run it for your organisation without publishing those changes under the AGPL.
- **Proprietary products and services**: you include SutraFlow, or parts of it, in a product, a hosted service (SaaS) or an appliance whose source you do not release under the AGPL.
- **Distribution without source**: you ship SutraFlow to customers without the source code.
- **Policy**: your organisation does not allow AGPL software, or needs warranties, indemnities or contract terms the AGPL does not give.

A commercial licence releases you from the AGPL's obligations for the scope agreed (for example a number of servers or engines, an organisation, a product), and may include support, maintenance and updates. Terms and price are agreed per customer.

**To ask for a commercial licence**, contact the copyright holder through [mixagile.com](https://mixagile.com), or open an issue titled *Commercial licence* on [the project's GitHub page](https://github.com/spinny007/SutraFlow).

They are not a derivative work of SutraFlow and are not covered by the AGPL or by this licensing model, whichever licence you use SutraFlow under. The same applies to the data your Sutras process.
